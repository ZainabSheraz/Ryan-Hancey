A trial lawyer, in the courtroom

I’ve tried cases for plaintiffs and defendants alike, and I prepare every one for a jury.

I’m a Kesler & Rust shareholder and a trial lawyer. I’ve built a successful and diverse litigation practice, representing and defending clients in all kinds of business and commercial disputes, contractual disputes, real estate and landlord-tenant litigation, products liability and personal injury matters, and before administrative agencies.

In just the past few months, I obtained six-figure jury verdicts in a breach of contract case against a municipality and a breach of franchise agreement case, as well as a $3.4 million verdict in a products liability case in federal court. During the same period, I successfully defended clients at trial against a breach of construction contract claim and a $10 million alienation of affections claim.

Away from the courtroom, I enjoy working out and playing basketball, and I’m an avid water skier.
